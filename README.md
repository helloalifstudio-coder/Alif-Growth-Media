## Our approach

```text
Understand the business
        ↓
Map the real workflow
        ↓
Design a usable solution
        ↓
Build & integrate
        ↓
Test, document & hand over
        ↓
Improve with feedback
```

**Client ownership matters.** Where appropriate, we structure deployments so the business can own its repository, cloud services, data, and access rather than depend on a shared agency login.
